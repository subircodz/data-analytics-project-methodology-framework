# Business Requirements

> **Document Type:** DAPM Phase 3

---

# Purpose

The Business Requirements phase identifies the information required by stakeholders to support business decisions.

The objective is to understand **what stakeholders need to know**, **why they need it**, and **how it will help them perform their responsibilities**.

This phase focuses on business needs, not technical implementation.

---

# When to Use

Perform this phase after completing **Stakeholder Analysis**.

Business Requirements should be completed before beginning **Data Discovery**.

---

# Phase Completion Summary

**Business Requirements are complete when:**

- Stakeholder information needs have been documented.
- Business questions have been identified.
- Required business metrics have been listed.
- Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) have been defined.
- Reporting requirements have been documented.
- Business rules have been recorded.
- Outstanding assumptions and clarifications have been identified.
- The project team has sufficient understanding to begin Data Discovery.
