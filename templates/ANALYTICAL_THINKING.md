# Analytical Thinking

> **Document Type:** Supporting Artifact

---

# Purpose

The Analytical Thinking document records the reasoning process followed throughout the project.

It captures ideas, assumptions, hypotheses, business questions, possible metrics, and analytical approaches before they are verified through data analysis.

This document represents the analyst's thinking process rather than the final conclusions.

---

# When to Use

Update this document throughout the project.

Analytical thinking may begin during Business Understanding and continue until the completion of EDA.

Ideas recorded here may later be confirmed, refined, or rejected based on evidence.

# Things to Remember

- Analytical thinking is not evidence.
- Assumptions must be validated.
- Hypotheses may be proved or rejected.
- Questions are allowed.
- Uncertainty is expected.
- Final conclusions belong in later phases.

---

# Phase Completion Summary

The Analytical Thinking document should evolve throughout the project.

By the end of the project:

- Important assumptions should be validated or rejected.
- Business questions should be answered.
- Analytical decisions should be documented.
- The document should provide a complete record of the analytical reasoning followed during the project.
