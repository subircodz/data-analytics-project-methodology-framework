# Observations

> **Document Type:** Supporting Artifact

---

# Purpose

The Observations document maintains a centralized record of factual observations identified throughout the project.

Observations are supported directly by business discussions, data profiling, validation, exploratory analysis, or other project activities.

This document acts as the project's evidence register.

---

# When to Use

Update this document throughout the project.

New observations may be added during any DAPM phase whenever new evidence is discovered.

# Observation Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| Open | Observation has been recorded and may be used in later phases. |
| Verified | Observation has been confirmed through supporting evidence. |
| Closed | Observation has been fully addressed or incorporated into the final deliverables. |

---

# Things to Remember

- Every observation must be supported by evidence.
- Observations describe facts.
- Observations should never explain why something happened.
- Observations should never suggest business actions.
- Duplicate observations should be avoided.
- Later documents may reference observations instead of repeating them.

---

# Phase Completion Summary

The Observation Register should grow throughout the project.

By the end of the project, every Business Insight and Recommendation should be traceable to one or more documented observations.
