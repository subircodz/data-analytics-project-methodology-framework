# Business Understanding

> **Document Type:** DAPM Phase 1

---

# Purpose

The Business Understanding phase develops a clear understanding of the business before any data analysis begins.

The objective is to understand how the organization operates, what problems exist, how success is measured, and why the project is important.

A good understanding of the business ensures that later analysis focuses on solving the right problem instead of simply analyzing available data.

---

# When to Use

Perform this phase immediately after completing the Project Brief.

Business Understanding should always be completed before Stakeholder Analysis, Business Requirements, or Data Discovery.

---

# Business Overview

Provide a high-level overview of the organization, department, product, service, or business domain being analyzed.

This section helps establish the overall business context.

# Business Process

Describe how the business currently operates.

Focus on the complete workflow from beginning to end.

Example:

- Customer places an order.
- Order is verified.
- Inventory is checked.
- Products are packed.
- Shipment is dispatched.
- Delivery is completed.

# Phase Completion Summary

**Business Understanding is complete when:**

- The business domain is clearly understood.
- The overall business workflow has been documented.
- Business objectives have been identified.
- Major business challenges have been recorded.
- Customers and business processes are understood.
- Initial business risks have been identified.
- The project team has sufficient understanding to begin Stakeholder Analysis.
