# Stakeholder Analysis

> **Document Type:** DAPM Phase 2

---

# Purpose

The Stakeholder Analysis phase identifies the people, teams, and departments involved in the business process.

The objective is to understand who performs each activity, who makes business decisions, who consumes analytical reports, and how different stakeholders contribute to the overall business workflow.

A clear understanding of stakeholders helps ensure that the analysis addresses the needs of the right people.

---

# When to Use

Perform this phase after completing **Business Understanding**.

Stakeholder Analysis should be completed before gathering Business Requirements.

# Phase Completion Summary

**Stakeholder Analysis is complete when:**

- All major stakeholders have been identified.
- Stakeholder responsibilities are clearly understood.
- Decision makers have been identified.
- Information providers have been documented.
- Information consumers have been identified.
- Stakeholder objectives have been recorded.
- The project team has sufficient understanding to begin gathering Business Requirements.
