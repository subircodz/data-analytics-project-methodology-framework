# Business Insights

> **Document Type:** DAPM Phase 9

---

# Purpose

The Business Insights phase interprets the findings from Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) within the business context.

The objective is to explain why the analytical findings are important and how they affect business performance, operations, or decision-making.

Business Insights should always be supported by evidence collected during EDA.

---

# When to Use

Perform this phase after completing **EDA Report**.

Business Insights should always be completed before preparing Recommendations.

---

# Insight Overview

Provide a brief summary of the overall business understanding gained from the analysis.

Focus on what the findings mean for the organization rather than describing the data itself.

---

# Business Insights

Document the insights derived from the analytical findings.

Each insight should explain the business impact of one or more observations.

# Insight Notes

---

# Phase Completion Summary

**Business Insights are complete when:**

- Analytical findings have been interpreted in the business context.
- Business opportunities have been identified.
- Business risks have been documented.
- Root causes have been identified where sufficient evidence exists.
- Every insight is supported by analytical evidence.
- The project team has sufficient understanding to prepare business recommendations.

---

# Next Step
