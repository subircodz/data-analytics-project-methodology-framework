# EDA Report

> **Document Type:** DAPM Phase 8

---

# Purpose

The Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) phase explores the validated dataset to answer the documented business questions.

The objective is to understand patterns, trends, relationships, distributions, and unusual behaviour within the data.

EDA focuses on discovering evidence from the data. It does not interpret business impact or recommend actions.

---

# When to Use

Perform this phase after completing **Data Validation**.

EDA should always be completed before Business Insights.
