# Recommendations

> **Document Type:** DAPM Phase 10

---

# Purpose

The Recommendations phase proposes practical actions based on the documented business insights.

The objective is to help stakeholders improve business performance by applying evidence gathered throughout the project.

Every recommendation should be supported by one or more business insights.

Recommendations should be practical, realistic, and aligned with the project objectives.

---

# When to Use

Perform this phase after completing **Business Insights**.

Recommendations should always be completed before preparing the Executive Summary.

---

**Recommendations are complete when:**

- Every recommendation is supported by business insights.
- Recommendations are practical and realistic.
- Expected business benefits have been documented.
