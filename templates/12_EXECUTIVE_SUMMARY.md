# Executive Summary

> **Document Type:** DAPM Phase 11

---

# Purpose

The Executive Summary provides a concise overview of the entire analytics project for business stakeholders.

The objective is to communicate the business problem, key findings, recommendations, and expected business value without requiring the reader to review the complete project documentation.

This document is written for decision makers rather than technical teams.

---

# When to Use

Prepare the Executive Summary after completing all previous DAPM phases.

This should be the final business deliverable of the project.

---

# Business Overview

Provide a brief introduction to the organization, business process, or problem addressed by the project.

Keep this section concise.

---

# Business Problem

Summarize the primary business problem or opportunity that initiated the project.
